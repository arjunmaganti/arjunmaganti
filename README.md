### Research

**[Drone-IQ](https://github.com/arjunmaganti/Drone-IQ)** — UC Irvine, IEEE AAIML 2026  
Developed and evaluated a drone-based building height estimation pipeline using DeepLabV3 segmentation and parallax geometry. Achieved 6.54% mean percentage error across evaluated buildings.

**[Co-occurrence of Extreme Heat and Pollution in the Southwest United States](https://github.com/arjunmaganti/Co-occurence-of-heat-and-pollution-in-the-Southwest-United-States)** — IJSCAR 2025  
Analyzed relationships between extreme heat, humidity, and atmospheric pollutants using historical climate reanalysis data.
